Recipes and brews

Record doses and results, follow live profiles and share your cup with photos.

On this page

  • Separate recipe from result
  • Weigh the dose with the portafilter
  • Time, curves and pours
  • Follow an espresso profile
  • Repeat and compare
  • Rate after tasting
  • Choose what to show and share

Separate recipe from result

Select coffee, lot and method before reviewing the recipe. Record dose, grinder and setting; add temperature, tools and water if they help you repeat it.

For espresso, ratio compares dose with beverage yield. For filter or immersion, it compares dose with water added. Final beverage and water added are not interchangeable.

Fictional examples: 18 g coffee and 36 g espresso give 1:2; 15 g coffee and 240 g filter water give 1:16.

Weigh the dose with the portafilter

  1. In Equipment → Portafilters, save the empty weight of the portafilter with its basket. You can save several combinations and choose the method’s default.
  2. In an espresso brew, open Weigh with portafilter and select the combination you are using.
  3. Zero the empty scale and weigh the portafilter with coffee. Enter the total weight or copy it from the connected scale.
  4. Review the subtraction and tap Use as dose.

Fictional example: 558 g total minus 540 g for the portafilter and basket gives an 18 g dose. Repeating a brew uses the portafilter’s current saved tare; check the combination if you have changed baskets.

Time, curves and pours

Enter times manually or use the timer. Without a connected scale, filter controls sit beside the water breakdown; with a scale, they are in the chart. Stop the timer at the end and review the result before saving.

The Pour assistant divides water between blooms and pours. Adjust dose, ratio, amounts and waits. A planned breakdown describes the recipe; a curve records the readings available from a device. Check which one you are viewing.

With a compatible scale, starting its timer opens the live chart. Wait for tare to finish before pouring. After a filter recording, also review the final beverage weight.

Reset weight and time stops the extraction and discards the current recording before returning to zero; confirm only if you want to start again. To complete older brews, Settings → Blooms and pours from curves fills missing details from usable scale curves while preserving existing phase counts and details.

Follow an espresso profile

  1. Prepare a new espresso and connect a scale for flow, a pressure sensor for pressure, or both.
  2. Before starting the timer, open Extraction assistant → Extraction profiles, choose a profile and tap Use for this extraction. You can also choose a saved shot as a reference. Applying a profile fills dose, temperature and, when available, predicted or target weight: review them for your coffee.
  3. Start the timer at the same point in the process as the reference. Time includes preinfusion.
  4. Follow the reference and live readings. With both curves, choose Combined or Separate views.
  5. Stop the extraction and review the data before saving. The brew retains the Profile used.

In the combined chart, flow uses the left axis in g/s and pressure the right axis in bar. The reference is dashed with circles; live readings are solid with diamonds. Gaps indicate missing readings.

Flow is calculated from weight reaching the cup; it is not the machine’s flow in ml/s. Tare, pauses and sudden load changes can interrupt the trace. The assistant is a visual guide: adjust and stop the machine manually. Learn how to create and edit profiles.

Repeat and compare

Use Repeat on a brew to start another with its recipe. Review the new cup before saving: repeating creates a new brew and consumes its dose.

Compare lets you choose another brew and inspect differences and curves without changing your journal. Missing values are not treated as zero. Differences show what changed, not which cup is better.

The app may also recall method values when you open a new brew. Always review prefilled fields.

Rate after tasting

Open Rate to record your impression, flavor notes and sensory profile. Coffee ratings and brew ratings are independent. Leave anything you have not evaluated unrated.

With dose, beverage yield and TDS, you can view extraction yield. Brix-based estimates and sensory-map references are guidance. Read the explanation in the app and compare it with your tasting.

Choose what to show and share

In Settings → Brew parameters, choose what appears in the form, details and history, and what is shared. Hiding a field does not erase its data.

Before sharing a brew, check the preview, selected fields and photo. Prepare your wording in Settings → Share templates. Sharing a cup is different from exporting a journal backup.

You can share text or an image and copy either to the clipboard. With Text over photo, place brew details or your template over a photo and adjust position, size, colour and Background opacity. Charts appear in separate panels over the photo so you can adjust the composition. Check readability in the preview.

Cards also support transparent backgrounds and light or dark text. The Days from roast tag is available in templates; check that the lot has a roast date.
