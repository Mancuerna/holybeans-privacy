Recipes and brews

Record what you did and use the result to decide what to repeat or change.

On this page

  • Separate recipe from result
  • Time, curves and pours
  • Repeat and compare
  • Rate after tasting
  • Choose what to show and share

Separate recipe from result

Select coffee, lot and method before reviewing the recipe. Record dose, grinder and setting; add temperature, tools and water if they help you repeat it.

For espresso, ratio compares dose with beverage yield. For filter or immersion, it compares dose with water added. Final beverage and water added are not interchangeable.

Fictional examples: 18 g coffee and 36 g espresso give 1:2; 15 g coffee and 240 g filter water give 1:16.

Time, curves and pours

Enter times manually or use the timer. Without a connected scale, filter controls sit beside the water breakdown; with a scale, they are in the chart. Stop the timer at the end and review the result before saving.

The Pour assistant divides water between blooms and pours. Adjust dose, ratio, amounts and waits. A planned breakdown describes the recipe; a curve records the readings available from a device. Check which one you are viewing.

Repeat and compare

Use Repeat on a brew to start another with its recipe. Review the new cup before saving: repeating creates a new brew and consumes its dose.

Compare lets you choose another brew and inspect differences and curves without changing your journal. Missing values are not treated as zero. Differences show what changed, not which cup is better.

The app may also recall method values when you open a new brew. Always review prefilled fields.

Rate after tasting

Open Rate to record your impression, flavor notes and sensory profile. Coffee ratings and brew ratings are independent. Leave anything you have not evaluated unrated.

With dose, beverage yield and TDS, you can view extraction yield. Brix-based estimates and sensory-map references are guidance. Read the explanation in the app and compare it with your tasting.

Choose what to show and share

In Settings → Brew parameters, choose what appears in the form, details and history, and what is shared. Hiding a field does not erase its data.

Before sharing a brew, check the preview, selected fields and photo. Prepare your wording in Settings → Share templates. Sharing a cup is different from exporting a journal backup.
