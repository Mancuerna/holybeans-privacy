Solve a problem

Start with the symptom and check the relevant step before changing your data.

On this page

  • I cannot find a coffee or brew
  • Stock does not match
  • A device is missing or sends no readings
  • Drive does not finish or my other phone differs
  • Photos are missing after recovery
  • The app cannot open the journal
  • Ask for help with useful context

I cannot find a coffee or brew

Clear the search, review filters and check archived entries. Archiving a coffee also archives its active brews. Restore the coffee to work with it again.

If this started after a restore, check the backup date and source. Save your current journal before restoring a different file.

Stock does not match

Check the lot, frozen portions and recent brew doses. Available and frozen are different amounts. Archiving a brew does not return its consumption.

To record an actual measured balance, use Adjust stock → Weighed correction. For a gift or loss, select its reason. Read the stock guide before correcting.

A device is missing or sends no readings

  1. Check that your version supports the model and that its battery is charged.
  2. Turn it on near the phone and disconnect it from other apps and phones.
  3. Check Bluetooth and Android's requested nearby-device permissions; some older versions need location permission for searching.
  4. Search again from Settings and check for readings.

If it connects but weight does not update, note the model and firmware. Different firmware can behave differently. You can keep recording weights manually. For printing errors, follow this check before repeating a job.

Drive does not finish or my other phone differs

Phones maintain independent journals. To recover another device's journal, select and restore a backup; there is no automatic merge.

If a backup fails, check your connection and account, return to the Drive screen and tap Back up now. Check the last completed backup date. Meanwhile, you can use your journal and export a ZIP.

If Google rejects authorization, keep a local backup and report the error message. Do not delete your journal to try to fix the connection.

Photos are missing after recovery

Check whether you restored a full ZIP or a JSON without images. Internal copies can only relink photos still on the phone. If you have a ZIP with photos, save the current state first, then use that file for recovery.

The app cannot open the journal

If Could not open your journal appears, close and reopen the app. The recovery screen lets you inspect error details and share copies saved before an update, when available.

Keep those copies before making changes; they do not include photos. Do not clear storage or uninstall as a troubleshooting step without an external backup. If the problem persists, report the version and error message following the contact guidance.

Ask for help with useful context

Include your HolyBeans version from Settings → About, Android version, steps taken, expected result and the message shown. For devices, include model and firmware if known.

Hide names, accounts, addresses and personal notes in screenshots. Do not attach passwords, keys, Bluetooth identifiers or a full journal backup for an initial question.
