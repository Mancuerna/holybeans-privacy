Coffees, lots and freezer

Keep track of what you have, what you have brewed and what you have set aside for later.

On this page

  • Organize coffees and lots
  • Purchased, available and frozen
  • Record gifts, waste or a weighed correction
  • Freeze and use portions
  • Archive a finished coffee

Organize coffees and lots

A coffee holds its name, roaster, origin and your notes. Use lots to distinguish purchases and roast dates. When brewing, choose the lot supplying the dose if several are available.

In Beans, search, filter and change the view. Grouping by roaster and the freshness calendar help you find bags; freshness estimates are guidance, not a measurement of taste.

Purchased, available and frozen

The purchased amount is a reference for buying and cost. Available coffee can be used without drawing from a frozen portion. Frozen stock is tracked separately.

Saving a brew with a dose deducts stock from the chosen source. Archiving a brew does not return coffee. When an amount is missing, the app keeps it unknown.

Record gifts, waste or a weighed correction

  1. Open Adjust stock from the coffee details.
  2. Choose Gift or Waste to remove an amount, or Weighed correction to set how much remains.
  3. Select the source, enter grams and review the resulting balance.
  4. Add a note if useful and save. View movements in Stock adjustments.

A gift or waste reduces inventory without counting as a brew. If stock was unknown, start with a weighed correction. Undoing an adjustment depends on the required coffee still being available.

Freeze and use portions

  1. Open Freeze coffee and choose the amount.
  2. For single doses, enable Divide into portions, enter a portion weight and decide what to do with the remainder.
  3. Use Thaw to return coffee to available stock. Use Thaw and brew to prepare a cup from a portion.
  4. Check the portion and dose before saving the brew.

Freezing changes where coffee is held, not how much you purchased. The brew retains its portion provenance.

Archive a finished coffee

Archiving preserves history and hides a coffee from the active list. It also archives its active brews without changing stock. Restoring the coffee brings those brews back; individually archived brews stay archived.

Use archive filters to find previous coffees. Before permanently deleting data, save a full backup.
