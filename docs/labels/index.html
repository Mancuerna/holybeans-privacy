Print labels

Design labels for coffees or frozen portions with compatible Niimbot and Pristar/Marklife P12 printers.

On this page

  • Check the model
  • Use continuous tape with the P12
  • Prepare the design
  • Connect and print
  • If printing is not confirmed

Check the model

Niimbot and Pristar/Marklife printing is experimental. Compatibility depends on model, firmware and paper. Select the exact model in HolyBeans; similar names are not interchangeable.

First prints have been confirmed with Niimbot D110, B1, B1 Pro and D11-H. D11-H has been tried with 12 × 40 mm paper; margins and other sizes need more validation. D11, B21 variants, B2 and other models without physical tests remain unverified.

Select D110_M for a printer advertised as D110_M or D110M. It is a different model from D110; its integration remains unverified on physical hardware in HolyBeans.

Use continuous tape with the P12

The Pristar/Marklife P12 uses 15 mm continuous tape with a 12 mm printable height. Choose a design length of 30, 40 or 50 mm; about 12.5 mm of extra tape feeds afterwards for manual cutting.

A legible 40 mm design has been confirmed. Other firmware, gap labels and other Marklife models are unvalidated. Disconnect the Marklife app before searching for the P12 in the label editor. Use the same template and image editor and always inspect the physical result.

Prepare the design

  1. Open the coffee menu and tap Print label.
  2. Select the exact model and installed paper size.
  3. Choose coffee or frozen-portion information and adjust the template.
  4. Review the preview, line breaks and text size. Check contrast if you include an image.

Labels are monochrome. A design that looks good on screen may need less content to remain readable on small paper.

The editor lets you adjust images horizontally and vertically. You can also include Days from roast in a template when the lot has that date. Recheck the preview after changing size or printer.

Connect and print

  1. Turn on the printer, check its paper and disconnect it from the Niimbot or Marklife apps and other phones.
  2. Search for and connect the printer from the label screen.
  3. Recheck model, paper and preview. Tap print when you are ready.
  4. Inspect the physical label even if the printer confirms the job.

HolyBeans sends a print job when you request it. It does not calibrate the printer, update firmware or change settings unrelated to the job.

If printing is not confirmed

First check whether a label came out. Check the cover, paper and battery before reconnecting. An error can mean the confirmation was lost even though the job printed.

Repeat the job only after checking the outcome. If the problem continues, note the model, firmware if known and paper dimensions when asking for help.
