Your first brew

Add a coffee, record a cup and save a backup of your journal. You can start without connecting any devices.

On this page

  • Add your coffee
  • Set up your equipment
  • Record the cup
  • A complete example
  • Save a backup

Add your coffee

  1. Open Beans and tap the add button.
  2. Enter the Coffee name. Add the roaster and any bag details you want to remember: origin, process or roast date.
  3. Enter the purchased amount and how much remains if you want to track stock. Leave a value blank if you do not know it; unknown stock is different from zero.
  4. Save the coffee. You can complete or correct its details later.

A coffee groups its information and brews; lots distinguish purchases or roasts of the same coffee.

Set up your equipment

In Equipment, add your brew method and, if you use one, your grinder. Choose the appropriate category: the app treats espresso output differently from water added to a filter brew.

Add tools and water profiles when you need them. A Bluetooth scale is optional: weights and times can also be entered manually.

Record the cup

  1. Open the coffee and choose Brew.
  2. Select a method and, if a selector appears, a lot. Choose your grinder or the pre-ground option.
  3. Enter the dose. For filter coffee, record water added; for espresso, enter beverage yield in the result.
  4. Record the time and any other details you need. Save the brew.
  5. After tasting, open Rate to add your rating and what you would change next time.

Save a backup

Go to Settings → Share backup and save the ZIP somewhere you can recover it. It includes your journal and photos. See Backups and restore to choose a backup routine.
