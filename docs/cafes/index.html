Coffee away from home

Record a café drink without creating a bag or changing your stock at home.

On this page

  • Record a drink
  • Find the venue
  • Review roasters and spending
  • Share only what you choose

Record a drink

  1. Open Cafés from its Home shortcut.
  2. Add a drink and select a venue or enter its details.
  3. Record the drink type and what you know about the coffee: roaster, origin or process.
  4. Add price and currency, a rating, notes or a photo if you want to keep them. Save.

Leave details blank if the venue does not provide them. The café's country and the coffee's origin are different things.

Find the venue

The picker includes a venue catalog and shows its source. Use it to fill in venue details; review the city and country before saving.

Recording a visit does not require your location. Opening a map link or website takes you to the corresponding service.

Review roasters and spending

Café drinks are kept separately from home brews and do not deduct coffee stock. Roaster profiles let you consult both parts of your history, distinguished from each other.

Café spending is grouped by currency: amounts in different currencies are not converted or added as though they were equivalent.

Share only what you choose

Open Share café drink and choose text or image. Review the included fields: dates, prices, notes and photos can reveal information you may prefer to keep in your journal.

Check the preview, then select an Android sharing destination. Sharing does not change the saved drink.
