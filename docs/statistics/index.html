Read your statistics

Understand the charts, explore your coffee year and choose cards to share.

On this page

  • Start with a date range
  • What each tab shows
  • Notice what is missing
  • Explore and share your coffee year
  • Explore your data in a spreadsheet

Start with a date range

Open Statistics and choose 7, 30 or 90 days, one year or all history. The range is shared across tabs.

Historical metrics include archived brews. Current stock follows a different rule: it reflects active coffee, including frozen stock, and does not depend on the selected period.

What each tab shows

Overview
Brews, consumption, dose, activity and stock. Consumption adds recorded doses, not gifts, waste or purging that you have not recorded as a brew.
Coffees
Origins, roasters, processes, varieties and ratings for coffees brewed during the period.
Brews
Methods, ratios, times, ratings and extraction measurements when enough data is available.
Costs
Purchases and estimated cost of coffee used, based on recorded quantities and prices.

Notice what is missing

A missing rating is not zero. A missing price prevents that cost from being calculated. Check how many records contribute information before comparing results.

Espresso and filter time and ratio summaries use medians. Espresso ratio uses beverage yield; filter ratio uses water. Average ratings reflect your records, not an objective measure of quality.

Tap a bar or point to inspect its value. Use Compare brews to examine two specific cups.

Explore and share your coffee year

From Statistics, open Your coffee year in review and choose a year. Browse stories about coffees, origins, roasters, methods, ratings and activity. About these numbers explains the records behind the recap; it includes archived and imported brews and does not equal the number of cups you drank.

Use Share card for one story or Share my year to select several cards and send a carousel. Review the selection: shared cards exclude photos and personal notes, but include the summary data they display. Recorded spending and estimated coffee cost are different measures.

Explore your data in a spreadsheet

In Settings, brew export to CSV lets you analyze records in a spreadsheet. It is an analysis export, not a full backup with photos or a restore file. To protect your journal, use a ZIP backup.
