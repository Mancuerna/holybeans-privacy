Equipment, water and connections

Organize your methods and connect devices when you want to record their readings.

On this page

  • Build your equipment list
  • Use the water calculator
  • Connect a scale
  • Pressure and refractometer
  • Read from a Meticulous

Build your equipment list

Equipment groups methods, grinders, tools and water. Add what you use. Associate tools with methods to find them when brewing; archiving equipment preserves its previous uses.

A water profile can be selected for multiple brews. The name used is recorded, but editing the profile composition later does not create a historical chemical measurement for each cup.

Use the water calculator

  1. Open Equipment → Water → Water calculator.
  2. Enter the starting water, final volume and recipe or target you want to explore.
  3. Review available salts and their chemical form, particularly calcium chloride.
  4. Check the calculated amounts and composition before saving the profile.

The calculator uses direct salt doses. Unknown starting-water values are not treated as zero; measured pH and TDS are not inferred from a recipe. References and their sources are available within the tool.

Connect a scale

  1. Turn on the scale and disconnect it from other apps or phones.
  2. Open Settings → Bluetooth scale → Find scale.
  3. Grant the device-search permission requested by Android and select your scale.
  4. Check that weight readings change before starting a brew.

You can remember several scales; HolyBeans maintains one active connection. If several are on, it prioritizes the last one used without replacing an existing active connection.

The app supports BOOKOO, with experimental support for DiFluid Microbalance (V300 or later), Microbalance Ti (V010 or later), and Acaia Lunar, Pearl, Pearl S and Pyxis. Model and firmware matter; being listed does not guarantee every feature on every unit.

Pressure and refractometer

Connect a BOOKOO Espresso Monitor in Settings → Bluetooth pressure sensor. HolyBeans starts and stops transmission to record pressure; it does not zero the sensor or change its settings.

Connect a DiFluid R2 in Settings → Bluetooth refractometer. The button beside TDS requests a measurement; readings started on the refractometer are also received. The app does not calibrate it or change its configuration. Both integrations are experimental.

Read from a Meticulous

Configure the machine connection in its brew method and use the shot-reading and import options. Your phone must be able to reach the machine over the configured network.

HolyBeans reads machine data; it does not start shots or change profiles or settings. Review the imported shot and its coffee association before saving. For labels, follow the printer guide.
