Backups and restore

Your journal is saved on your phone. Choose how to keep another copy and understand what restoring does.

On this page

  • Save a full backup with photos
  • Distinguish internal copies from the daily folder
  • Enable Google Drive backups
  • Understand what restoring does
  • If you are coming from BeanConqueror

Save a full backup with photos

  1. Open Settings → Share backup.
  2. Choose where to save or send the ZIP through Android's share sheet.
  3. Check that the file reached its destination. Keep a copy outside the phone if you want to recover it after losing the device.

The ZIP includes the journal and photos. A CSV is for analysis, and a standalone JSON does not carry image files. Keep the ZIP for a full backup.

Current backups include the extraction profile library and the reference used for each shot. During a manual export or restore, wait for progress to finish before starting another operation. An error is not a completed backup: check the destination file.

Distinguish internal copies from the daily folder

Internal backups
Help recover the journal on the same device. They do not contain an independent photo backup and are lost when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
Daily copy folder
Choose it in Settings through Android's picker. It stores full ZIPs with photos and keeps the 14 most recent daily copies. A Home reminder offers to save when there are changes; you can also tap Save a copy now.

Folder copies remain after uninstalling HolyBeans, but a folder on the same phone does not protect against losing that phone. If Android no longer permits access, select the folder again.

Enable Google Drive backups

  1. Open Settings → Google Drive backup.
  2. Tap Enable with Google and choose the account you want to use.
  3. Tap Back up now and check the last completed backup date.

Drive tries to back up changes when you open the app or move it to the background. You can keep using your journal during uploads and offline. Saving a brew does not guarantee a new Drive backup already exists.

The latest five backups from each device are kept. These are independent backups: journals on two phones do not merge or update each other automatically. Android may interrupt an upload when the app closes; check the date when you need to be sure it finished.

Drive uses HolyBeans' private app storage, not your other documents. Turning backups off keeps existing copies. See the privacy policy for disconnection and deletion details.

Understand what restoring does

Restoring replaces the entire journal on this phone; it does not just add missing entries. First export a ZIP of the journal you want to keep.

  1. For a file, open Settings → Restore backup and select the ZIP.
  2. For Drive, open Browse backups to restore and choose a copy by date and device.
  3. Review the summary and confirm only if it is the journal you want to recover.
  4. Afterwards, check coffees, brews and photos.

Drive restoration first saves and verifies a local safety copy and preserves the destination's local settings. You can also select versions from the previous synchronization system.

If you are coming from BeanConqueror

Use the dedicated Import from BeanConqueror option in Settings and follow its file-preparation instructions. Review the summary before confirming.

This import adds data through its own workflow; it is different from restoring a HolyBeans backup. Save a ZIP first and review stock, photos and brews afterwards.
