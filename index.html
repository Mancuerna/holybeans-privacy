Privacy Policy

How HolyBeans handles your data.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

HolyBeans is a coffee tracking application designed to help users manage coffee beans, brews, equipment, tasting notes, and related statistics.

Data collection

HolyBeans does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data or user-generated coffee data with the developer or third parties.

Information entered into the app — including coffee beans, brewing records, equipment, tasting notes, statistics, and related data — is stored locally on the user's device.

HolyBeans does not require users to create an account.

Local data

Application data is stored locally on the user's device and is used only to provide the app's functionality. The developer does not have access to this data.

Users can remove locally stored data by deleting it within the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling HolyBeans.

Third-party services

HolyBeans does not use advertising services and does not share user data with third parties.

Data security

Because HolyBeans does not transmit user data to the developer's servers, user data remains under the user's control on their device.

Children's privacy

HolyBeans is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if HolyBeans' data handling practices change. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the email address published on the HolyBeans listing on Google Play.

This version assumes HolyBeans stores data locally and does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud sync, or other services that transmit user data.
