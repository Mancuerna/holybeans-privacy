Journal, backups and synchronization

You can use the local journal without a HolyBeans account. Google Drive synchronization is optional and requires your authorization and a compatible account. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Use the same Google account on devices that should share a journal, and wait for synchronization to finish before switching devices. When different versions exist, review which journal you choose to keep: selecting a version may replace the entire active journal. Keep independent backups of information you want to preserve.

Disabling Drive does not delete remote data. Google services’ availability, capacity and operation depend on their terms and your account’s status. Synchronization alone does not guarantee recovery from every loss or modification.
