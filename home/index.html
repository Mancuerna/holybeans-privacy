Local by default. Drive when you choose.

Use HolyBeans without creating an account. Your journal is stored on your device, and you can export your own backups.

If you enable Google Drive, HolyBeans requests access only to its private app data storage to synchronize your journal and photos across your devices. It cannot access your other Drive documents. Connecting is optional and can be disabled in Settings.

Read what is stored and how to delete it in our Privacy.

Connect your coffee equipment

Record readings and curves from compatible equipment, use water tools and compare your brews. Availability depends on your device and its compatibility with HolyBeans.
